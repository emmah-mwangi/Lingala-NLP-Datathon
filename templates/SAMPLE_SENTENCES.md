# Lingala Intent Classification – 30 Sample Sentences

**Team:** 7 – Datalynk  
**Language variety:** Kinshasa Lingala  
**Status:** Draft for linguistic and label review

These examples are initial drafts. They must be reviewed before being accepted as dataset contributions.

## 1. weather_query

| Lingala sentence | English meaning |
|---|---|
| Mbula ekobeta lobi? | Will it rain tomorrow? |
| Lelo moyi ekozala makasi? | Will the sun be strong today? |
| Lobi mopepe ekopela makasi? | Will the wind blow strongly tomorrow? |

## 2. datetime_query

| Lingala sentence | English meaning |
|---|---|
| Ngonga ezali boni? | What time is it? |
| Lelo mokolo nini? | What day is it today? |
| Tokomi na mokolo ya boni? | What date is it? |

## 3. alarm_set

## 4. calendar_set
