# Volunteer Consent Notice — Lingala NLP Datathon
Team 7 (Datalynk) | Strathmore NLP Hub Datathon 2026 | Track A: Intent Classification

---

## 1. What this project is

Team 7 (Datalynk) is building a Lingala-language dataset for intent classification as part of the Strathmore NLP Hub Datathon 2026. The dataset will be used to train and evaluate a machine-learning model that recognises the intended action in a spoken or written Lingala sentence (for example: asking about the weather, setting an alarm, or querying a list).

The project is an academic exercise. It is not a commercial product and does not involve any live system that acts on user requests.

---

## 2. What you are being asked to contribute

If you agree to participate, you will be asked to write approximately 16–17 short, natural Lingala sentences, each illustrating one of the ten intent categories listed in the project taxonomy. You will receive a copy of the authoring guidelines (VOLUNTEER_GUIDELINES.md) before you begin.

You will not be asked to record audio, share personal opinions, or provide any information about yourself beyond what is described in section 5.

---

## 3. Participation is voluntary

Taking part is entirely your choice. You may decline to participate, stop at any point, or withdraw a contribution after submission, subject to the limitation described in section 7. There is no penalty for declining or withdrawing.

---

## 4. How your contribution will be used

Your sentences will be:

- Reviewed by a team member for quality and label accuracy.
- Included in a CSV dataset used to train and evaluate the intent classifier.
- Attributed in the dataset using a pseudonymous author code (see section 5), not your real name.
- Cited in the team's contribution statement using your pseudonymous code only.

The dataset may be submitted to the Strathmore NLP Hub as part of the team's final deliverable.

---

## 5. Pseudonymous author codes

To protect your privacy, you will be assigned a pseudonymous code (T07-V1, T07-V2, or T07-V3). This code will appear in the dataset's `contributor_code` column and in any public documentation. Your real name, student number, phone number, email address, and any other identifying information will never appear in the public dataset or in any public repository.

A private record linking your code to your identity is held separately by the team lead and is never committed to any public or shared repository.

---

## 6. Public dataset distribution — separate explicit choice

The dataset produced by this project may be released publicly (for example, on a public GitHub repository or a data repository such as Hugging Face) after the datathon, subject to organiser and team decisions.

**You must indicate separately whether you give permission for your contributed sentences to be included in any such public release.**

- [ ] I give permission for my contributed sentences (identified only by my pseudonymous code) to be included in a publicly released version of this dataset.
- [ ] I do not give permission for public release. My sentences may be used for the datathon submission only and must be removed before any public release.

This choice must be confirmed explicitly. The default is no public-release permission until you confirm otherwise.

---

## 7. Withdrawal and its limitations

You may request withdrawal of your contribution at any time before the dataset is frozen for final evaluation. To withdraw, contact the team lead (Emmah, T07-B) through the agreed team channel.

**Limitation:** Once the dataset has been frozen, the trained model artefact cannot be updated to remove the influence of withdrawn records without retraining. The team will make reasonable efforts to remove your records from any public release if you withdraw before freezing.

---

## 8. What must never appear in your contributions

Your sentences must not contain:

- Real names of any person (including yourself).
- Phone numbers, email addresses, or any contact details.
- Student registration numbers or admission numbers.
- Real addresses, ID numbers, or any other personally identifying information.
- Sensitive personal information about any individual.

If a sentence you submit accidentally contains any of the above, notify the team lead immediately so it can be corrected or removed before review.

---

## 9. Consent confirmation

**This document does not record your consent.** Consent must be confirmed separately through the agreed process (for example, a signed form, a dated message in the team channel, or another method agreed with the team lead).

No contribution will be accepted or attributed until consent has been confirmed.

To confirm consent, a volunteer must:

1. Read this document in full.
2. Make an explicit choice about public-release permission (section 6).
3. Confirm both to the team lead through the agreed channel.

The team lead will record the confirmation date and method in the private consent tracking log.
