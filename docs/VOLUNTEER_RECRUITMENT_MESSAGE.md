# Volunteer Recruitment Message
Team 7 (Datalynk) | Prepared by T07-B (Emmah)

The message below is a template for WhatsApp or similar channels. Adapt the greeting and sign-off to your relationship with the recipient. Send it individually, not as a group broadcast, so each person can respond privately.

---

## Recruitment message (WhatsApp)

> Hi [name],
>
> I hope you're doing well! I'm reaching out because I'm working on a Lingala NLP (Natural Language Processing) project as part of the Strathmore NLP Hub Datathon 2026, and I'm looking for Lingala speakers who might be willing to help.
>
> **What the project is:**
> We're building a small dataset of natural Lingala sentences to train a model that recognises what someone is asking for — things like checking the weather, setting an alarm, or asking for a definition. It's an academic project, not a commercial product.
>
> **What I'm asking for:**
> If you're interested, you'd write approximately 16–17 short, natural Lingala sentences — the kind of thing you might say to a voice assistant. It should take around 20–30 minutes. I'll send you clear guidelines before you start.
>
> **A few important things:**
> - Participation is completely voluntary. You can say no or stop at any time.
> - Your name will never appear in the dataset. You'd be identified only by a private code (like V1 or V2).
> - Before you contribute anything, I'll send you a short consent notice explaining exactly how your sentences will be used and asking for your explicit agreement.
> - You'll also be asked separately whether you're happy for your sentences to be included if the dataset is released publicly after the datathon.
>
> Would you be open to hearing more, or would you like me to send you the consent notice to read first?
>
> Thanks so much — no pressure at all if it's not for you!
>
> [Your name / Emmah]

## Notes for the recruiter (Emmah)

- Do not record anyone as a confirmed volunteer until they have read the consent notice and explicitly confirmed consent.
- Do not share the consent notice as a public post. Send it individually.
- Record each person's response (interested / declined / no reply) in your private notes. Do not record this in any file committed to the repository.
- Once a volunteer confirms consent, assign their pseudonymous code (T07-V1, T07-V2, or T07-V3) and update the private consent tracking log.
- Target: 3 volunteers. T07-V1 and T07-V2 contribute 17 records each; T07-V3 contributes 16 records (total: 50 test-set records).
- Recruitment is not complete until all three volunteers have confirmed consent and received the authoring guidelines.
